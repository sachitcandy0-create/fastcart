Chicken Masala is a hand-blended spice powder made from premium whole spices that are sun-dried, roasted and ground in small batches. The result is a rich, aromatic masala that gives your food an authentic, home-cooked flavour every single time.

Key Features:

Made from naturally sourced spices, free from artificial colours and preservatives. It adds the perfect balance of heat, aroma and taste to curries, rice, snacks and marinades. Hygienically packed to lock in freshness.

How To Use:

Add the recommended quantity while cooking or as directed on the pack. Adjust to taste. Using a dry spoon always helps to keep the powder fresh and free from lumps.

Storage:

Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Keep away from moisture and direct sunlight. Best consumed within 6 months of opening.
